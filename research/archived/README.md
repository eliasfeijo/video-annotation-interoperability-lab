# Archived Research Documents

Documents in this directory are **preserved and version-controlled**. They are
historically useful but are **not the primary current-state research surface**.

- **Preserved:** every file is tracked by git; no history is deleted.
- **Historically useful:** each document owns unique provenance (experiment records,
  process audits, superseded plans, inventories, checklists) that remains cited
  by the canonical synthesis.
- **Not primary surface:** the current conceptual, normative, and publication
  surface lives directly under `research/` and is navigated via
  `research/current-state-index.md` (L6).

**Archival does NOT mean:** obsolete, false, deleted, untrustworthy, or
scientifically irrelevant. Frozen experiment reports, decision records, and phase
audits remain the evidence backing the capstone. Their conclusions are summarized
in `research/capstone.md` §12, but the reports themselves remain the owning
sources for their era.

**Navigation entry point:** `research/current-state-index.md` (L6) — pointer/index
only; owning documents win. See also `research/research-program.md` for the
living program and `research/capstone.md` for the canonical synthesis.

**Governance and mutability** remain defined by `research/consolidation-map.md`
(L0–L6 layers, preservation constraints, N6 edit flow). Archived documents retain
their original mutability regimes (frozen, append-only, controlled).

**Reference integrity:** live navigational links on current surfaces have been
updated to `research/archived/<file>` where needed. Historical textual citations
inside frozen documents remain historically faithful and are not rewritten.
